# Alpstein AI — Business Goals

## Main Business Goal

The main business goal of Alpstein AI is to create a recurring revenue product for small and medium-sized businesses.

The product should help businesses avoid losing leads and reduce manual communication work.

## Value Proposition

Alpstein AI helps businesses:

- respond to customers faster;
- avoid missed messages;
- collect leads automatically;
- save customer information;
- reduce repetitive communication;
- look more professional;
- work 24/7 without hiring extra staff.

The core message:

"Never miss a customer message again."

## Target Customer

The first target customers are local small businesses in Switzerland, such as:

- restaurants;
- barbershops;
- shawarma shops;
- beauty salons;
- small shops;
- service providers.

These businesses often use WhatsApp or direct messages for communication and do not always have a structured CRM process.

## First Sales Strategy

The first sales strategy is local direct outreach.

The founder will:

- create a working demo assistant;
- create simple brochures with a QR code;
- visit local businesses personally;
- demonstrate the assistant live;
- collect feedback;
- offer a pilot setup.

The first goal is not scale. The first goal is to get real feedback and find a repeated business pain.

## First Customer Goal

The first business milestone is to get one real business using the assistant.

Success means:

- the assistant is connected to a real or demo communication channel;
- the business owner understands the value;
- the assistant handles real customer-style messages;
- leads are saved;
- the owner receives notifications.

## Revenue Model

The future revenue model is monthly subscription.

Possible pricing:

- Basic: 49 CHF/month;
- Standard: 99 CHF/month;
- Pro: 199 CHF/month.

At the MVP stage, pricing can be flexible. The goal is to validate willingness to pay.

## Business Metrics

Important early metrics:

- number of businesses contacted;
- number of demos shown;
- number of interested businesses;
- number of pilot users;
- number of leads captured by the assistant;
- response accuracy;
- business owner satisfaction;
- willingness to pay.

## Short-Term Goals

The short-term goals are:

1. Build a working demo.
2. Show the demo to local businesses.
3. Get first feedback.
4. Connect one pilot business.
5. Improve the assistant based on real conversations.

## Medium-Term Goals

The medium-term goals are:

1. Get 3–5 pilot businesses.
2. Identify the best niche.
3. Create reusable assistant templates.
4. Improve onboarding.
5. Start charging monthly subscription.

## Long-Term Goals

The long-term goals are:

1. Build a self-service AI assistant platform.
2. Allow businesses to configure assistants themselves.
3. Support multiple communication channels.
4. Build recurring monthly revenue.
5. Expand from local businesses to a wider SMB market.

## What This Project Is Not

Alpstein AI is not:

- a generic chatbot toy;
- a large enterprise CRM;
- a complex multi-agent research project;
- a dashboard-first product;
- a product built only for developers.

It is a practical business tool for customer communication and lead capture.

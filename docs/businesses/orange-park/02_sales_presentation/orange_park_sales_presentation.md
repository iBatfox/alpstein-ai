# Orange Park Sales Presentation

Present Orange Park in a calm, consultative way.

## Main advantages

- Near Kyiv with more space, greenery and a quieter environment.
- Closed territory and car-free yards for family comfort.
- White Box preparation reduces the amount of rough renovation work.
- Residential, commercial and daily infrastructure in one complex.
- Formats for living, family needs and investment.

## Buyer angles

- Families: safety, car-free yards, childcare and nearby education.
- First purchase: White Box and general financing paths.
- Moving from Kyiv: suburban comfort with transport access.
- Investors: property format, purpose and current manager-confirmed options.
- Commercial buyers: facade premises, access, technical requirements and use.

Answer stable facts first, mention one or two relevant advantages, then ask one
qualification question. Do not request contact before it is useful.
